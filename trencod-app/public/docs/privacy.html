Information stored by the app

  • Account: chosen username, user ID, salted password hash and hashed session records. Do not use your real name or reuse an important password.
  • Creations: code, art, animations, supported music, visual scenes, saved versions and project metadata.
  • Community: Studio memberships, team messages, comments, follows and related activity.
  • Marketplace: demo-coin balances, transaction records, listings and ownership/license records.
  • Support: report text, category, your account ID, dates, status and the owner's current reply.

Who can see it?

Published games, public creator profiles and public community activity are visible to visitors. Browser-delivered game code can be inspected. Unpublished personal projects are restricted by application permissions. Shared Studio content is accessible to its members. Support reports are visible to their author and configured support owners. Site operators and infrastructure administrators may have database access; private does not mean end-to-end encrypted.

Cookies and local drafts

A session cookie keeps you signed in for up to seven days. Editors may keep recovery drafts and preferences in your browser. On a shared device, sign out and clear site data after saving/exporting work; clearing site data can remove unsaved drafts.

Hosting and outside services

The deployed service can use Render and an external PostgreSQL database such as Neon. Their processing and logs depend on the owner's accounts and configuration. Python practice downloads Pyodide components from an external CDN. If connected, AI coaching sends the prompt you submit to the configured AI provider. Do not include secrets. Games may request resources from third-party websites.

Retention, exports and deletion requests

This release does not promise an automatic deletion schedule or provide automatic account deletion. You can download your personal project export while signed in. This export contains projects, personal art, visual scenes and music; it is not a complete account-data export and does not include team chat or all transaction records.

Use Account feedback to ask the owner about correction or deletion. Requests require manual review; submitting one does not automatically delete records. Backups may retain earlier data until the operator removes or expires them. The owner must establish a retention and deletion process before making stronger promises.

Young creators

Use a made-up username. Never put your address, phone number, school details or passwords into a game, public comment, AI prompt or support report. Ask a trusted adult for help with account and privacy decisions.
