Demo coins, not real money

Trencoins (TC) are beta demo credits with no cash value. This release does not offer cash payouts or make an investment promise.

Before listing

Publish your own original game, select it in Creator Market, set the price and choose creator-credit terms. A listing stores a source snapshot. If you edit the original later, update the listing to sell the new snapshot. Do not list stolen work.

When a game sells

  1. The server checks the active listing and buyer balance.
  2. Payment, receipt, ownership transfer and listing closure happen in one database transaction.
  3. The buyer receives the project as a private draft in My projects. The seller loses edit and publish access to that project.
  4. The updated market checks the price and credit terms shown to the buyer before charging.

Old non-exclusive licenses keep their original terms. New exclusive sales do not erase copies previously downloaded or independently stored. Exact duplicate checks are not plagiarism detection. Standalone source resale of purchased projects is restricted by the platform.

If something goes wrong

Check My projects, Creator Market → Your purchases and your wallet history. Avoid repeatedly buying when a request times out. Report the listing/project ID, time, expected price and observed result through Purchase feedback. Never send your password. This release has no automatic refund/dispute-resolution system; the owner must investigate.
